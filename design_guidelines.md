# Design Guidelines for LightDreams Landing Page

## Design Approach
**Reference-Based Approach**: Marketing landing page for "Santuario del Sueño Infantil" requiring deep emotional connection with overwhelmed parents. Design emphasizes warmth, natural rhythm, and sanctuary-like comfort to build trust and drive app downloads.

## Core Design Elements

### A. Color Palette
**Primary Colors:**
- Linen White: 43 33% 97% (main background, cards)
- Sage Green: 81 12% 68% (primary brand, navigation)
- Peach: 18 75% 90% (accent, warm highlights)
- Graphite Gray: 220 6% 26% (text, strong contrast)

**Supporting Colors:**
- Pure White: 0 0% 100% (button backgrounds, overlays)
- Soft Sage: 81 20% 85% (subtle backgrounds)
- Deep Peach: 18 60% 80% (hover states)

### B. Typography
- **Primary Font**: Playfair Display (Google Fonts) - elegant, emotional headlines
- **Body Font**: Nunito Sans (Google Fonts) - warm, highly readable
- **Headings**: Regular to Bold (400-700) for gentle authority
- **Body Text**: Regular weight (400) with generous 1.6-1.8 line height

### C. Layout System
**Tailwind Spacing Units**: 4, 6, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24
- **Sections**: py-16 to py-24 for breathing room
- **Cards**: p-8 to p-12 for sanctuary-like comfort
- **Flow Elements**: gap-6 to gap-12 for natural rhythm

### D. Component Library
**Navigation**: Sage green header with Playfair Display logo, minimal menu, soft CTA
**Hero Emocional**: Large Playfair headline, nurturing subtext, phone mockup on peach gradient
**La Promesa**: Three benefit cards with gentle icons on linen backgrounds
**El Rostro Humano**: Founder story with authentic photo and personal message
**Solución en Bloques**: Feature grid with app screenshots and explanatory text
**Prueba Social**: Parent testimonials in elevated sage-tinted cards
**CTA Final**: Warm invitation with app store badges on gradient background

**Interactive Elements:**
- Rounded corners (rounded-xl, rounded-2xl) for organic feel
- Subtle shadows (shadow-md, shadow-lg) for gentle elevation
- Soft transitions on hover states
- Blurred button backgrounds when overlaying images

### E. Visual Treatment
**Gradients**: Linen-to-peach gradients in hero and CTA sections for warmth
**Backgrounds**: Organic textures, soft sage accents for sanctuary atmosphere
**Spacing**: Generous whitespace mimicking peaceful, uncluttered nursery
**Imagery**: Natural lighting, authentic family moments, app interface in organic frames

## Images
**Large Hero Image**: Phone mockup showcasing app interface positioned right side of hero section with subtle peach gradient background
**Founder Photo**: Authentic, warm portrait for "El Rostro Humano" section
**Feature Screenshots**: 3-4 app interface captures in organic phone frames
**Family Lifestyle**: Peaceful parent-child moments for testimonial backgrounds
**App Store Badges**: Official download buttons styled with brand colors

## Content Strategy
**6-Section Emotional Journey:**
1. **Héroe Emocional**: Immediate empathy with sleep-deprived parents
2. **La Promesa**: Clear value proposition with gentle authority
3. **El Rostro Humano**: Personal connection through founder story
4. **Solución en Bloques**: Practical benefits demonstration
5. **Prueba Social**: Parent success stories and testimonials
6. **CTA Final**: Warm invitation to join the sanctuary community

Design maintains empathetic, sanctuary-like atmosphere while ensuring clear conversion pathways through gentle, trust-building visual hierarchy.
